SPECIALS (facing right)
Every fighter has 3 specials on the same motions:
↓↘→ · →↓↘ · ↓↙← + P or K
SUPERS: ↓↘→↓↘→ + K = Lv1 (1 bar) · + P = Lv2 (2 bars) · + P+K = ULTRA (3 bars)
Each fighter also has a → + button command normal
CHARGE fighters (Volt, Rosa): hold ← or ↓ ~⅔ s, then →/↑ + button
SYSTEM
Hold back to block · down-back blocks lows · overheads must be blocked standing
Throw: LP + LK up close · Dash: double-tap ←/→
Move lists: character select & pause screens
COMBOS · Normals chain L → M → H on hit or block (lights chain into lights) · any normal cancels into a special or super · specials cancel into supers on hit · Lv1 supers juggle — keep comboing or cancel into a bigger super
AIR COMBOS · ↓+HP launches · hold ↑ as it hits to super jump · chain air attacks (up to 5) · finish with air HP/HK to slam them down
e.g. ↓LK → ↓MP → ↓HP, ↑, j.MP → j.MK → j.HK
